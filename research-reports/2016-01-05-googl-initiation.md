# Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL): Initiation

**PFW-SIF Equity Research · Communication Services team**
**Published:** January 5, 2016 · **Rating:** Buy · **Price at pitch:** ~$742 · **12-month target:** $900
**Investment committee:** Approved, January 7, 2016 · **Executed:** January 11, 2016 at $733.07

> Facts in this report are limited to what was public on the publication date. Prepared for the PFW-SIF student investment fund (simulated portfolio). Educational only; not investment advice.

## Recommendation
We recommend initiating a position in Alphabet Class A shares. Search is shifting to mobile without losing its economics. A new holding structure and a new CFO are bringing cost discipline that the market has not yet priced in.

## The business today
- Q3 2015 revenue of **$18.7 billion**, up 13% (about 21% in constant currency). Paid clicks rose about 23%, led by mobile search and YouTube.
- In October 2015 Google reorganized into **Alphabet**, separating the core Google business (Search, Ads, YouTube, Android, Maps, Cloud, Play) from "Other Bets" such as the self-driving car project, Verily (life sciences) and Google Fiber. Other Bets will be reported separately starting with Q4 2015 results.
- CFO Ruth Porat, who joined in May 2015, has emphasized expense discipline. Alphabet announced its **first share buyback (~$5.1 billion)** in October 2015.
- Cash and marketable securities of about **$73 billion**, with no meaningful debt.

## Why we would buy now
1. **Mobile is additive, not a threat.** Concern that mobile ads would earn less than desktop ads is easing: mobile query growth is driving paid-click growth, and Android and Chrome secure distribution.
2. **YouTube.** YouTube is the leading destination for online video, and brand advertising is moving from TV to online video.
3. **Transparency and discipline.** Separate reporting will show how profitable core Google is once Other Bets losses are stripped out. We expect that to support a higher valuation.
4. **Reasonable price.** Roughly 22x forward earnings, or less net of cash, for a business growing in the mid-teens with very high returns on capital.

## Risks
- **Regulation.** The European Commission's antitrust case over Google Shopping is ongoing.
- **Other Bets spending.** These businesses lose billions a year, and the timing of any payoff is uncertain.
- **Ad-blocking and pricing.** Cost-per-click continues to fall as the mix shifts to mobile and emerging markets.
- **Currency.** A strong dollar reduces reported growth.

## Valuation and target
A 24x multiple on our 2016 earnings estimate, plus net cash, implies about **$900** per share, roughly 21% upside.

## Committee vote
Approved, January 7, 2016. At the same meeting, the committee declined the Intel pitch, preferring Alphabet's higher growth.
