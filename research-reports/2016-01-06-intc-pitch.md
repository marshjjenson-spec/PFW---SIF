# Intel Corporation (INTC): Pitch (not approved)

**PFW-SIF Equity Research · Information Technology team**
**Published:** January 6, 2016 · **Rating:** Buy · **Price at pitch:** ~$33 · **12-month target:** $40
**Investment committee:** Not approved, January 7, 2016

> Facts in this report are limited to what was public on the publication date. Prepared for the PFW-SIF student investment fund (simulated portfolio). Educational only; not investment advice.

## Pitch summary
Intel is a cash-rich leader with a growing data center business, trading at a discount because of the shrinking PC market. The team argued that data center and the newly closed Altera acquisition would offset PC weakness.

## Key facts at the time
- Q3 2015 revenue of **$14.5 billion**. The **Data Center Group** earned $4.1 billion in revenue, up 12%.
- Closed the **$16.7 billion Altera** acquisition in late December 2015, adding programmable chips (FPGAs) for data center and networking.
- Quarterly dividend of **$0.24** (about a 3% yield). Shares were near **13x** trailing earnings.
- The PC market declined about 10% in 2015 by unit volume.

## Bull case presented
- Server CPU market share above 95%.
- Manufacturing leadership on 14nm, with 10nm expected next.
- A dividend and buybacks backed by strong cash flow.

## Why the committee declined
- **PC exposure.** Over half of revenue still came from the shrinking PC market.
- **Mobile failure.** Intel had spent billions on contra-revenue subsidies and still lacked a position in smartphones.
- **Manufacturing risk.** The schedule for the next process node was slipping. The committee saw a risk that Intel's manufacturing edge would narrow.
- **Opportunity cost.** At the same meeting, the committee preferred Alphabet's faster growth for the available capital.

The fund tracks this pitch's return against the S&P 500 from the pitch date, so the decision can be judged over time.
