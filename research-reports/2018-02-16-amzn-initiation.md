# Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN): Initiation

**PFW-SIF Equity Research · Consumer Discretionary team**
**Published:** February 16, 2018 · **Rating:** Buy · **Price at pitch:** ~$1,448 · **12-month target:** $1,850
**Investment committee:** Approved, February 22, 2018 · **Executed:** February 26, 2018 at $1,521.95

> Facts in this report are limited to what was public on the publication date. Prepared for the PFW-SIF student investment fund (simulated portfolio). Educational only; not investment advice.

## Recommendation
We recommend initiating a position in Amazon. Investors still judge Amazon on its thin reported earnings. We think Amazon is really two leading businesses, a retail platform and the largest cloud provider. A third, advertising, is growing quickly and will lift margins.

## The business today (full year 2017)
- Net sales of **$177.9 billion**, up 31%. Net income of **$3.0 billion**.
- **Amazon Web Services:** revenue of **$17.5 billion**, up 43%, and operating income of **$4.3 billion**. AWS earned more operating income than the whole company.
- "Other" revenue, mostly advertising, was about **$4.7 billion**, up roughly 58%.
- Trailing free cash flow of about **$8.4 billion**.
- Acquired **Whole Foods Market** for $13.7 billion in August 2017, adding about 460 stores and a grocery foothold.

## Why we would buy now
1. **AWS is a category leader with operating leverage.** Enterprises are only beginning to move to the cloud. AWS has a multi-year lead in breadth of services and scale.
2. **Advertising is high-margin and early.** Shoppers search Amazon with purchase intent, and sponsored product ads monetize that traffic with very little added cost.
3. **Prime flywheel.** Prime members buy more, more often. Every new benefit, such as video, faster shipping and Whole Foods discounts, raises retention.
4. **Cash flow, not EPS.** Amazon reinvests heavily, so reported earnings understate earning power. On free cash flow and segment profits the valuation is easier to defend.

## Risks
- **Valuation.** About 100x trailing GAAP earnings leaves little room for disappointment.
- **Spending.** Heavy investment in fulfillment, video and international markets could keep margins low longer than expected.
- **Regulation and politics.** Amazon's size in retail draws growing political scrutiny.
- **Cloud competition.** Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud are spending aggressively to catch up.

## Valuation and target
A sum of the parts gives about **$1,850** per share, roughly 25% upside. We value AWS at 20x 2019 operating income, retail and advertising at 1.5x 2019 sales, and add net cash.

## Committee vote
Approved, February 22, 2018. At the same meeting, the committee declined a General Electric turnaround pitch.
