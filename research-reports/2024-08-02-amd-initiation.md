# Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD): Initiation

**PFW-SIF Equity Research · Information Technology team**
**Published:** August 2, 2024 · **Rating:** Buy · **Price at pitch:** ~$132 · **12-month target:** $185
**Investment committee:** Approved, August 5, 2024 · **Executed:** August 6, 2024 at $130.18

> Facts in this report are limited to what was public on the publication date. Prepared for the PFW-SIF student investment fund (simulated portfolio). Educational only; not investment advice.

## Recommendation
We recommend initiating a position in AMD after a roughly 40% pullback from its March 2024 high. AMD's data center business doubled year over year last quarter. Its MI300 accelerators give AI buyers a credible second source next to Nvidia, and the share price now discounts much of the near-term uncertainty.

## The business today (Q2 2024, reported July 30)
- Revenue of **$5.8 billion**, up 9%.
- **Data Center** revenue was a record **$2.8 billion**, up 115%. **MI300** accelerator sales passed **$1 billion** in a single quarter for the first time.
- Management raised its 2024 data center GPU revenue outlook to **more than $4.5 billion**.
- Client (Ryzen PC) revenue rose 49% to $1.5 billion. Gaming and Embedded fell sharply as those markets work through excess inventory.
- Non-GAAP gross margin was **53%**. Q3 revenue guidance is about **$6.7 billion**.
- Announced the acquisition of **Silo AI** in July 2024 to strengthen its AI software and model expertise.

## Why we would buy now
1. **AI accelerators are supply-constrained, and customers want a second source.** Hyperscalers are deploying MI300X, and AMD has committed to an annual product cadence.
2. **EPYC server share gains.** AMD keeps taking server CPU share on performance per watt. That supports data center growth even outside AI.
3. **The PC cycle is turning.** AI-capable PCs and an aging installed base support a client recovery.
4. **Entry price.** The broad market selloff in early August pushed AMD to about $130, roughly 40% below its March high. That gives a better margin of safety.

## Risks
- **Nvidia's lead.** CUDA's software ecosystem is deep. AMD's ROCm software must keep improving for customers to switch.
- **Supply.** Advanced packaging and high-bandwidth memory capacity could cap MI300 shipments.
- **Export controls.** Restrictions on AI chip sales to China limit the addressable market.
- **Valuation and volatility.** Even after the decline the stock trades at a premium multiple and swings with AI sentiment.

## Valuation and target
About 35x our 2025 earnings estimate implies roughly **$185** per share, ~40% upside, with a higher outcome if data center GPU revenue keeps beating guidance.

## Committee vote
Approved, August 5, 2024, during the market selloff. Bought at the next day's close.
