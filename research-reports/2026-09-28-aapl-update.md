# Apple Inc. (AAPL): Position review, why we continue to hold

**PFW-SIF Equity Research · Information Technology team**
**Published:** September 28, 2026 · **Rating:** Hold · **Held since:** January 4, 2016

> Prepared for the PFW-SIF student investment fund (simulated portfolio). Returns are total returns (dividends reinvested) from the purchase date through October 2, 2026, from Tiingo end-of-day data. Educational only; not investment advice.

## Result so far
Apple has returned about **+1,309%** since purchase, against **+356%** for the S&P 500 Total Return over the same period. It is the fund's largest position.

## How the original thesis played out
- **Installed base and Services.** This was the core of the 2015 thesis, and it worked better than expected. Services became a business of more than $100 billion a year with gross margins far above hardware, and now makes up a much larger share of profit than in 2015.
- **Capital return.** Apple kept buying back stock on a large scale, cutting its share count substantially since 2016. That boosted per-share earnings beyond revenue growth.
- **The "peak iPhone" fear** was right about unit volumes but wrong about value. Higher prices, wearables and services offset flat unit sales.

## Why we hold rather than add or sell
- **Hold:** the ecosystem, pricing power and cash generation remain intact. Selling would trigger a large realized gain in a real account, and nothing in the thesis has broken.
- **Not adding:** the valuation is now well above the ~11x earnings we paid. Growth depends more on Services and on whether Apple can deliver competitive AI features. Regulation of the App Store in the US and EU is a direct risk to Services margins.
- **Position size:** Apple has grown to about 40% of the fund through appreciation. The committee will review trimming it against the fund's position-size limit.

## What would change our view
A sustained decline in Services growth, a forced change to App Store economics, or evidence that AI features are shifting upgrade demand to rival platforms.
