# Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD): Position review, why we continue to hold

**PFW-SIF Equity Research · Information Technology team**
**Published:** September 28, 2026 · **Rating:** Hold · **Held since:** August 6, 2024

> Prepared for the PFW-SIF student investment fund (simulated portfolio). Returns are total returns (dividends reinvested) from the purchase date through October 2, 2026, from Tiingo end-of-day data. Educational only; not investment advice.

## Result so far
AMD has returned about **+387%** since purchase, against **+51%** for the S&P 500 Total Return over the same period. It is the fund's best performer relative to the market.

## How the original thesis played out
- **Second source for AI.** The 2024 pitch argued that AI buyers wanted an alternative to Nvidia. That has happened: large customers signed multi-year accelerator deals with AMD, including OpenAI's commitment in October 2025 to deploy AMD Instinct GPUs at multi-gigawatt scale.
- **Annual roadmap.** AMD kept its yearly accelerator cadence (MI325, MI350 and the MI400 series with full rack-scale systems). It also bought ZT Systems to design complete AI server racks.
- **EPYC** server CPUs kept gaining share, as expected.

## Why we hold rather than add
- The thesis is working, but the price now reflects much of it. The stock is close to five times our purchase price, so new money buys much less margin of safety.
- AMD is highly sensitive to AI spending. A slowdown in hyperscaler capital spending would hit it harder than the other holdings.
- Nvidia remains the leader, and AMD's software ecosystem still trails.

## What would change our view
Customers cancelling or delaying accelerator deployments, or AMD falling behind Nvidia's product cadence.
