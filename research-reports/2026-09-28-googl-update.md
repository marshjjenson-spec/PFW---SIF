# Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL): Position review, why we continue to hold

**PFW-SIF Equity Research · Communication Services team**
**Published:** September 28, 2026 · **Rating:** Hold · **Held since:** January 11, 2016

> Prepared for the PFW-SIF student investment fund (simulated portfolio). Returns are total returns (dividends reinvested) from the purchase date through October 2, 2026, from Tiingo end-of-day data. Educational only; not investment advice.

## Result so far
Alphabet has returned about **+846%** since purchase, against **+377%** for the S&P 500 Total Return over the same period.

## How the original thesis played out
- **Mobile search and YouTube** grew as expected, and **Google Cloud** went from an afterthought in 2016 to a large, profitable segment.
- **Cost discipline and buybacks** arrived as the 2016 pitch hoped. Alphabet added a regular dividend in 2024 alongside large repurchases.
- **Other Bets** remain a small cost center, with Waymo as the most advanced.

## Why we hold
- **AI is now the central question.** Generative AI chat tools challenged the idea that Search is untouchable. Alphabet responded with its Gemini models and AI features in Search, and its own TPU chips give it a cost edge in running AI. So far Search revenue has kept growing.
- **Regulatory risk** is real but has been manageable. US courts found Google held an illegal search monopoly, but the 2025 remedies stopped short of a forced breakup or a Chrome sale.
- At current prices, we see a fair balance between AI-driven upside and the risk of Search disruption. That supports holding, not adding.

## What would change our view
Clear evidence of Search share loss to AI assistants, or remedies that end Google's default-search payments in a way that hurts traffic.
