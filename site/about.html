About PFW-SIF

Real research. Real discipline. Every result in public.

The Purdue Fort Wayne Student Investment Fund is a student-run equity portfolio. Students research companies, pitch them to an investment committee, manage the positions the committee approves, and report the results against the S&P 500 Total Return. Every pitch, including the ones the committee turns down, is published so the fund's judgment can be measured over time.

Mission

Why the fund exists

Learn by doing

Practice the full job

Members do the work professional analysts and portfolio managers do: build models, write research, defend a thesis in front of a committee, size positions, and explain results they cannot change after the fact.

Accountability

Measured against the market

Every number is compared with the S&P 500 Total Return, with dividends included. Beating a weak benchmark teaches nothing; the fund reports the hard comparison.

Transparency

Nothing is hidden

Pitches, committee votes, trades, holdings, risk statistics and the methodology behind them are all published, including the ideas the committee rejected and how they did afterward.

How it works

From idea to position

The full process, policy limits and sell discipline are on the Investment process page.

1 · Sector teams

Research

Students are organized into sector teams aligned with the S&P 500's GICS sectors. Each team covers its sector and develops pitch ideas.

2 · Pitch

Present

A team writes an initiation report with a thesis, valuation, price target, catalysts and risks, and presents it to the investment committee.

3 · Committee

Decide

The investment committee questions the team and votes. Approved ideas are bought at the next close; declined ideas are recorded and tracked.

4 · Monitor

Report

Positions are reviewed at least once a year against their original thesis. Results update every trading day on the Performance and Holdings pages.
