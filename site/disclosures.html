Disclosures

The Purdue Fort Wayne Student Investment Fund is an educational program. This site reports a simulated portfolio.

Simulated performance

Unless a page states otherwise, performance shown on this site is simulated. It is calculated from a paper portfolio using actual historical closing prices, dividends and stock splits, and does not represent the results of actual trading or assets under management. Simulated results have inherent limits: they do not reflect trading costs beyond any commissions recorded, market impact, taxes, or the effect of real-money decisions under pressure, and parts of the record were reconstructed after the fact. Past performance, simulated or actual, does not guarantee future results.

Retrospective research

Research reports dated before the fund's launch are retrospective case studies. They are written using only information that was public on the report date, and the investment committee votes recorded with them are part of the simulation.

Not investment advice

Nothing on this site is investment, legal, tax or accounting advice, an offer to buy or sell any security, or a recommendation that any security is suitable for any person. Ratings and price targets are student opinions produced for education. Do your own research and consult a licensed professional before investing.

Methodology and data

The portfolio is valued at each market close from the trade ledger. Monthly returns use the Modified Dietz method; longer-period returns compound monthly returns. The benchmark is the S&P 500 Total Return, measured with SPY adjusted closing prices. Risk statistics use monthly returns and the risk-free rate shown on the Performance page. Prices, dividends and splits come from Tiingo; company financials in research reports come from SEC EDGAR filings. Data may contain errors or delays, and figures may be restated if source data is corrected.

Affiliation

PFW-SIF is a student organization at Purdue University Fort Wayne's Doermer School of Business. Views on this site are those of the student members and do not necessarily reflect the views of the university or its faculty.

Privacy

This site does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not collect personal information. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and read public fund data from the fund's database. Your browser may remember small display preferences locally.
