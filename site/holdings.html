Prices & market value

Last price is the official closing price on the date shown at the top of the page, from Tiingo end-of-day data. Market value is shares held multiplied by the last price. Portfolio weight is market value divided by total portfolio value, including cash.

Cost basis & gains

Average cost is the total paid for the shares currently held, including commissions, divided by those shares. When shares are sold, the earliest purchases are treated as sold first (FIFO). Unrealized gain is market value minus that cost. Dividends are credited to cash on the ex-dividend date and are not included in a position's unrealized gain.

Sector classification & benchmark weights

Sectors follow the Global Industry Classification Standard (GICS).

Policy limits

Limits come from the PFW-SIF investment policy and are checked against weights at the last closing price. A position can drift past a limit through price moves alone; the policy then requires the committee to review it at its next meeting.

Dividend income & yield

Income received is the sum of cash dividends credited since inception. Estimated annual income multiplies each position’s shares by its trailing 12-month dividends per share. Portfolio yield is estimated annual income divided by total portfolio value, including cash.

Realized gains

Realized gain is sale proceeds, net of commissions, minus the average cost of the shares sold. Return is realized gain divided by that cost and excludes dividends received while the position was held.

Beta

Position beta is measured from daily total returns against SPY over the trailing 252 trading days and requires at least 60 days of history. Portfolio beta is the weighted average of position betas, with cash at zero, and is shown only when every holding has a beta.
