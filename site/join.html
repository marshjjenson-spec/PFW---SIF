Membership

Learn Investing By Doing It, In Front Of A Committee.

Members research real companies, write reports, pitch to the investment committee and help manage the fund's portfolio. Anyone can join: Purdue Fort Wayne students, students from other schools, any major, any level of experience. There is no stock pitch to get in. We talk with you about your background, help you get started, and you work alongside the team from day one.

What you get

Why Students Join

Real Analyst Work

Financial modeling, valuation, industry research and written reports, the core skills of equity research and asset management jobs.

A Public Track Record

Your reports are published on this site with their price targets and results, something concrete to show employers.

Presentation Practice

Pitching to the committee and defending a thesis under questioning builds the skills interviews test.

Leadership

Members can move up to sector lead and officer roles that run the portfolio, risk and operations.

Expectations

What Members Do

Every week

  • Attend the weekly fund meeting (about one hour).
  • Work with your sector team on coverage and research (two to four hours).
  • Follow news on the companies your team covers or holds.

Every semester

  • Contribute to at least one initiation report or position review.
  • Present a pitch or review to the investment committee.
  • Take part in the annual review of the holdings your team covers.

How to join

Four Steps

Reach Out

Any time

Tell us a little about yourself: your school, your major or field, and what interests you about investing. No stock pitch, no résumé and no prior experience required.

Conversation

About 20 minutes

A relaxed talk with fund officers about your background, your goals and the sectors you are curious about. There are no trick questions and no finance quiz; it is how we find the right place for you.

Onboarding

First two weeks

We help you get started: you join a sector team, learn the fund's research template and valuation model, and sit in on an investment committee meeting.

Work With The Team

From day one

You research companies with your sector team, contribute to reports and position reviews, and build up to presenting your own pitch to the committee when you are ready.
