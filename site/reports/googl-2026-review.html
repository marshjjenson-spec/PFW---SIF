Investment thesis

AI is the central debate; we hold

Alphabet has returned about 846% since we bought it, against 377% for the S&P 500 Total Return. The 2016 thesis, mobile search, YouTube and cost discipline, played out, and Google Cloud grew from an afterthought into a large, profitable segment.

Capital returns arrived as we hoped. Alphabet now pays a regular dividend, introduced in 2024, alongside large buybacks.

The central question now is generative AI. Chat assistants challenge the idea that Search is untouchable. Alphabet has responded with its Gemini models and AI features in Search, and its own TPU chips give it a cost advantage in running AI.

Why we continue to hold Alphabet

What went right

Every part of the 2016 thesis worked: mobile search monetized well, YouTube became a major advertising business, and the Alphabet structure brought cost discipline and capital returns. Cloud was a bonus we did not model.

The AI question

The biggest risk to Search in two decades has arrived. So far, Search revenue has kept growing as Google added AI answers. Alphabet has one of the strongest AI research teams and its own chips, so it is a likely winner of the shift, not only a target of it.

Why not add

The stock has re-rated as investors gained confidence in Alphabet's AI position. At today's price, the balance between AI upside and Search disruption risk looks fair.

Position size

At about 31% of the fund, Alphabet is a large position. Together with Apple, the fund is heavily concentrated in two technology platforms. The committee will review this alongside the Apple trim.

What would change our view

Clear evidence that AI assistants are taking Search share, or remedies that end Google's default-search agreements in a way that reduces traffic.

DCF cross-check

On base-case assumptions (cloud and AI growth; free cash flow is depressed today by data center spending), the DCF gives $104 per share, 70% below the $343.50 price. The market price assumes faster or longer growth than our base case. That gap is a main reason we hold rather than add. A DCF is sensitive to its inputs, so we use it as a check on the target, not the target itself.
