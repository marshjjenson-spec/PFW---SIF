Research process

From idea to position

Every stock the fund owns has gone through the same five steps.

  1. Idea generation

    Sector teams screen their sector for companies with a mispricing worth investigating.

  2. Due diligence

    Analysts study the business, its competitors and management, and build a valuation model.

  3. Written report

    The team writes an initiation report with a thesis, risks, a price target and a recommendation.

  4. Committee vote

    The team pitches to the investment committee, which approves or rejects the position and its size.

  5. Monitoring & exit

    Holdings are reviewed with updates as news arrives; an exit memo records why a position is sold.

NotesHow research results are measured Show notesHide notes
Return since pitch

Total return of the stock, with dividends reinvested, from the closing price on or before the report's publication date to the latest close shown at the top of the page. It is measured the same way whether or not the fund bought the stock, so it reflects the quality of the idea rather than the fund's trading.

Versus the S&P 500

The S&P 500 Total Return (SPY, dividends reinvested) over exactly the same dates. The difference is shown in percentage points. A return is shown as N/A when no closing price is available within a week of the publication date, or when the report was published on the latest price date.

Price at pitch & upside

Price at pitch is the price stated in the report when one was recorded; otherwise it is the closing price on or before the publication date. Upside at pitch is the price target divided by the price at pitch, minus one.

Track record

The track record includes only Buy-rated pitches with a committee decision (approved or not approved) and a measurable return. Averages are simple averages across pitches, not weighted by position size. Approval rate is approved pitches divided by all decided pitches. Exit memos, sector reviews and pending pitches are listed in the library but are not part of the track record.

Prices

Closing prices are Tiingo end-of-day data, refreshed after each U.S. market close.
