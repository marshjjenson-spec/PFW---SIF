Governance

The investment committee

Who votes

The Chief Investment Officer (chair), the Portfolio Manager, the Chief Risk Officer, the Director of Research and the sector team leads. The faculty advisor attends and may comment but does not vote.

How a vote works

The pitching team presents and takes questions. A simple majority of members present approves. Members of the pitching team do not vote on their own pitch. Every vote is recorded with the report.

What it decides

New positions, additions, trims and exits; changes to the investment policy; and the annual review of every holding against its original thesis.
